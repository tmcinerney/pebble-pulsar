The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added
- Initial release of Pulsar Timer for Pebble OS.
- 10 Quick duration presets (1m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 25m Pomodoro, 30m, 45m, 60m).
- 10-Dot micro-LED progress depletion gauge.
- Pebble `Wakeup API` background wake integration for background alarm firing.
- Ascending 3-2-1 warning countdown beeps.
- Escalating ruby alarm firing screen with 8-bit synth chime and high-frequency vibration.
- Quick add/subtract (+1m / -1m) during active countdown.
- 7 Vintage LED colorways and 12° Italic slant support.
- Clay configuration framework integration.
